# Tier 1 - review of the remaining 42 variants

23 September 2026. **22 additional PNG adaptations are ready; 20 remain drafts.** All 42 received a separate drawing revision and an explicit source/render review. This does not complete Tier 1.

Current mandatory coverage: **229 ready / 276 slots**, **20 drafts**, **27 without a matching 2026/27 reference**. Global current coverage: **247 ready**, **28 drafts**, **1,195 researching**. The 317 saved archive references and 249 exact-season mandatory references are unchanged; 233 archive adaptations are now reviewed.

## Scope and evidence

The fixed scope is `artifacts/footballkitarchive/tier1-remaining-42.json`, taken only from the 42 drafts in the prior 71-variant batch. All 735 original IDs and both slots per team are preserved. Primary evidence is the independently saved Football Kit Archive **2026/27** Home or Away image; no 2026-only kit was substituted. Per-team equipment facts remain local JSON and are attached to applied revisions.

Source-page rechecks and saved-image provenance: [source audit](../data/research/tier1-42-2026-09-23-source-checks.json). Thirteen relevant detail pages were retrieved again; Oțelul returned a fetch error. Other page checks were not repeated: their already saved exact-season records and images were inspected. The failed Oțelul request is not presented as a successful fresh lookup.

## Drawing and review

New revision files preserve the previous drawings and every already reviewed revision. The original 8000 × 5000 PNG, its folds, alpha and sewn round collar remain unchanged. Editable vector colors and prints are adapted to that base. No source photograph is embedded, and no logo, sponsor, badge, lettering, V-neck or polo flap is deliberately drawn. HEX values are visual estimates, not official specifications.

The work replaces generic texture fragments with selected source-derived ink contours and redraws geometric fields where crop repetition was unsuitable. Collar/cuff ornaments follow the actual PNG seam curves. Some printed fragments are repeated or reflected to fit the shared shirt; these are flat source-based adaptations, not exact copies of the photographed garment. Thirty-five crop specifications, source hashes and diagnostic previews are retained in [trace provenance](../data/research/tier1-42-2026-09-23-traces.json). Crops and full renders were inspected; early trial crops containing branding or collar pieces were corrected before saving final revisions.

The authoritative visual review is in eleven [source/render sheets](../artifacts/footballkitarchive/tier1-42-2026-09-23/comparison-1.png), numbered 1–11, and [42 explicit decisions](../data/research/tier1-42-2026-09-23-review.json). Unfinished artwork remains unavailable through Apply.

## Reproduce

```sh
node scripts/research/fka-tier1-42-2026-09-23.mjs --reviewed
node scripts/research/fka-batch-review.mjs tier1-42-2026-09-23
node scripts/research/fka-import.mjs
npm run catalog:build
```

The reviewed mode checks every saved review decision and refuses changes to approved revisions. Further corrections to ready kits require a new revision. Test/build results are recorded in [validation](VALIDATION.md).
