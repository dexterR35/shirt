## Adaptation limits

The original 8000 × 5000 PNG remains the sole fabric and shape source. SHA-256: `4670b8b8dd8d66d22a4b5313aac9e5406d9c0913244a72bdcebe41e347637042`. Validated vector colors, bands and artwork are composited with its RGB and original alpha. The physical round collar is recolored only; source polo flaps, V-necks, plackets and Mandarin construction are not drawn over it. Colors are visual estimates, not official published HEX values.

Comparison review corrected stripe positions, shoulder widths, false fold-derived patterns and broken ink masks. Reșița Home's apparent dark diagonal fold was removed from the paint. Clermont Home's central stripe and Sochaux Away's ink separation were corrected before promotion. Logos, sponsors, manufacturer symbols, club emblems and lettering are omitted, including Padova's repeated animal marks and Vicenza's large monogram.

Fine graphics are adaptations to the shared silhouette. Guingamp's spirals and Nancy's chevrons use simplified editable geometry. Dijon uses clean fleur-de-lis contours. Grenoble's Away mountains retain their hatched form with reduced fine-line resolution. Reims Away V2 scales a clean lower-front floral field across the torso, with separate sleeve samples. Red Star's fine shoulder maze and white-on-white details are simplified. Sponsor-obscured patches on Dunkerque, Nantes, Palermo, Hellas Verona and Sochaux are reconstructed from explicitly recorded clean areas; exact hidden artwork is not claimed. Each revision's observations and trace manifest record its own crops, exclusions, repairs and limitations. No source-photo raster or continuous photo shading is embedded in the paint.
