NETBET SPORT — DEFAULT JERSEY r003
13 October 2026 | Design proposal; UX Lead approval pending

DESIGN AND REFERENCE
Based on the user's NetBet Sport promotional-banner reference: navy surfaces,
teal slanted panels with rounded corners, and mint highlights. Flat screenshot
pixels sampled at #0A1225 (navy), #49D3AA (mint), #1E1E1E (surround).
Teal varies across a gradient. Shirt paint values are adapted for the original
fabric multiplication and small-size visibility, not claimed official tokens.
No athlete imagery, promotional text, brand lettering, club badge, sponsor,
flag or national symbol appears on the garment. Colours refer to NetBet's
visual identity rather than a researched team. Similarity to every existing
team kit cannot be excluded; this design is an original generic fallback.

SOURCE AND EDITABILITY
Original base.png: 8000 x 5000. Its fabric detail, silhouette and physical
round collar are retained. Five validated vector shapes form overlapping
rounded teal panels, mint panel trim and sleeve/shoulder paint. The existing
collar ring is recoloured; no extra collar is drawn. Native alpha is unchanged.
editable-paint/*.config.json and *.svg contain editable paint layers.
The SVGs are paint sources, not standalone finished jersey assets. Final
fabric-preserving assets are transparent PNG and lossless WebP.
Rebuild: node scripts/build-netbet-generic-jersey.mjs
Paint source: src/lib/generic-jersey-netbet.js

SURFACES
on-dark uses brighter teal sleeves and lighter navy for dark navy/charcoal
components. on-light uses deeper navy and teal for white/light components.
Choose by the immediate component background, not just the global theme.
Do not apply a colour filter, reduce opacity, crop or stretch the image.
Keep object-fit: contain and a fixed square slot to avoid layout shifts.
Use a 2x source for the CSS size: 24->48, 32->64, 40->80, 48->96,
64->128, 80->160, 168->336. A 24 CSS px minimum is proposed.
Use 3x sources where available, e.g. 24->80, 32->96, 48->160, 80->256.
Smaller slots and photographic backgrounds require separate review.

USAGE CONTRACT — DEVELOPMENT IMPLEMENTS SEPARATELY
Mapped club jersey: retain it. Unmapped club: use this generic jersey.
Mapped national flag: retain it. Missing national flags are outside this scope.
If adjacent text already names the team, use alt="". Otherwise expose the
team name accessibly. Never use the fallback as an indication of selection,
live status, an actual team kit or a verified kit season.
No fallback logic or team mappings are included in this delivery.
