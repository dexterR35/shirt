# Tier 1: review of the remaining 71 source-backed variants

23 September 2026. All 71 scoped Home/Away variants now have a new saved drawing revision, an inspected source/render comparison and an explicit review decision. **29 passed; 42 remain drafts.** This is not completion of Tier 1.

Current catalog: **225 ready globally; 207 / 276 mandatory slots ready**. Mandatory gaps are 42 source-backed drafts and 27 slots without exact 2026/27 evidence. Global status: 225 ready, 50 draft, 1,195 researching. The 27 reference gaps remain Real Sociedad Home/Away, Petrolul Away and the 24 Finnish slots with calendar-year 2026 evidence only.

This batch uses the independently saved Football Kit Archive 2026/27 references already on disk; it does not claim a new scrape or new evidence for missing slots. All 735 local per-team equipment JSON records remain available. Competition metadata does not exclude a team. The original 8000 × 5000 PNG and sewn round collar are retained; source colors are estimates, and source collar construction is deliberately adapted to that ring. No logos, sponsors, lettering or emblems are added. Monaco ASM monograms and Paderborn club crests/initials/year figures are deliberately omitted.

## Reproduce and resume

The fixed scope is `artifacts/footballkitarchive/tier1-remaining-71.json`. The explicit decisions are `data/research/tier1-71-2026-09-23-review.json`. New native geometry is split into country modules under `scripts/research/tier1-71`. All 71 current revisions and local references are linked from `artifacts/footballkitarchive/tier1-71-2026-09-23/manifest.json`. Reviewed revisions are immutable; later corrections need a new revision/batch.

```powershell
node scripts/research/fka-tier1-71-2026-09-23.mjs --reviewed
node scripts/research/fka-batch-review.mjs tier1-71-2026-09-23
node scripts/research/fka-import.mjs
npm run catalog:build
```

The comparison script produces 18 contact sheets and 71 individual PNG adaptations. All 18 sheets were inspected, including a second pass after density, geometry, trim and clipping corrections. Formal checks and real-browser application results are recorded in [VALIDATION](VALIDATION.md).
