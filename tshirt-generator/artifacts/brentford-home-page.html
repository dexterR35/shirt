Printing Disclaimer

Personalisation of Replica Kit

Brentford Football Club reserves the right to refuse any personalisation that is considered offensive, abusive, discriminatory, or otherwise inappropriate.

Printing

Please note that sizing may vary between different replica kits. We strongly recommend ensuring you select the correct size before adding personalisation, as we are unable to offer refunds or exchanges once names and/or numbers have been applied.

Brentford Football Club cannot accept responsibility for any changes to player names or numbers due to transfers, squad updates, or a player changing their number or leaving the Club.

The standard printing charge includes up to 12 characters, however this may vary depending on shirt size, as not all sizes can accommodate the maximum character limit. All printing is subject to availability, and our team can advise on options before purchase. Please note that any changes or amendments may delay your order.

Care Instructions for Personalised Shirts

Personalised shirts may appear creased upon arrival due to packaging during transit or folding.

To remove creases safely, place a clean, damp cloth over the personalisation and gently press with a warm iron. Do not iron directly onto the print.

Washing Instructions

To help maintain the quality of your shirt and personalisation, always follow the care label instructions inside the garment.

We recommend washing the shirt inside out at a cold temperature. Do not tumble dry or iron directly over printed areas.

Brentford 26/28 Adult Authentic Home Shirt

Official Brentford FC Merchandise

Celebrating our history and Future

For the first time, Brentford's iconic red and white stripes are accompanied by an eye-catching, honey yellow detail on the collar, cuffs, side panels and surrounding the crest and partner logo. This authentic kit is created with a jacquard weave with intricate designs which have been woven into the fabric, helping elevate performance. This technique and the micro-mesh ventilation panels keep the kit lightweight and breathable, so the players body's stay dry, even during peak performance.

In addition to its contemporary design, the shirt includes a subtle nod to Brentford's history with beehive detailing on the back of the neck, reflecting the club's old quadrant crest, which was used from 1993 to 2017, when the present-day badge was introduced. Blending traditional design with a modern twist, this season's home kit offers a fresh take on a timeless classic. 

Materials, features & care instructions
  • Material 1: 100% Recycled Polyester Jacquard
  • Material 2: Mesh

  • Material 4: 100% Recycled Polyester Interlock 
  • Silicone Printed Brentford Crest 
  • Official Silicon Joma tag 
  • Made in China

Click & Collect / Store Collection

Please read the information below carefully before arriving to collect your order.

If you are collecting on a matchday, please visit the Brentford Superstore, Lionel Road South, Kew Bridge Distribution Centre, TW8 9QR.

If you are collecting on a non-matchday, please go to the Hive Store, Unit 1, Marsden House, 4 Lionel Road South, Brentford, TW8 0RY.

When collecting your order, you will need to present your order confirmation email and photo ID.

If someone is collecting on your behalf, please contact our support team in advance with the name of the nominated person. They will also be required to bring valid photo ID.

Orders must be collected within 72 hours of receiving your collection confirmation email. Any uncollected orders after this time will be cancelled and refunded to the original payment method. Please note that personalised items are non-refundable.

Phone Orders

Returns Policy

Items can be returned within 28 days of purchase for a refund or exchange, provided they are in a resalable condition and accompanied by valid proof of purchase.

We are unable to offer refunds or exchanges on items that are returned after 28 days, unless they are confirmed to be faulty.

Sale items may be exchanged but are not eligible for refunds or credit notes.

Faulty or Incorrect Items

If you believe your item is faulty or you have received an incorrect item, please contact our Online Shop team at [email protected] or visit The Hive Store with your item and proof of purchase.

We may request additional information or images to help resolve your query as quickly as possible.

Exchanges

Exchanges are subject to stock availability. Items must be returned in a resalable condition along with proof of purchase.

If you are unable to visit the store, items can be returned by post to The Hive Store. Please include your order details and contact information inside the parcel.

Refunds

Refunds will be processed back to the original payment method where possible. Please allow time for your refund to be completed once your return has been received and processed.

We reserve the right to refuse refunds or exchanges if items are found to be worn, damaged, tampered with, or not in a resalable condition.

Personalised items are non-refundable and non-exchangeable, unless faulty. This does not affect your statutory rights.

Returning Your Item

We strongly recommend using a tracked or signed-for delivery service when returning items, as we cannot accept responsibility for parcels lost in transit.

Once your return has been received, please allow 5–7 working days for it to be processed. If further information is required, a member of our team will contact you using the details provided.

If you have not heard from us after this time, please contact our Customer Services team with your order details.
